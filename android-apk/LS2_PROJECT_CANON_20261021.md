## Identity and boundaries

LS-2 is a transitional region of silent storage frames, incomplete labels, derelict clockwork structures, and a set of corridors whose floor marks are difficult to interpret. Four main zones and four subzones per zone are implemented in `Level0AreaWorldDefinition.kt`: **Thư Viện Mép Trống**, **Xưởng Đồng Hồ Rỗng**, **Hành Lang Sai Số**, and **Nút Chốt Không Tên**. The full zone/subzone descriptions in the catalog are the canonical descriptions.

- A missing label is **not** automatically a number, a code, or a route.
- Clockwork and irregular tapping noises do **not** prove time travel, accelerated time, or a temporal anomaly.
- Door frames, latches and alignment marks do **not** guarantee an exit before the player has completed Core verification.
- The area does not spawn a guaranteed NPC, Entity or quest item just to provide a puzzle answer.

## Mechanics, not narrative shortcuts

Two multi-step Core puzzles enforce verification rather than guessing: **Dãy nhãn thiếu dữ kiện** and **Nhịp đếm và chốt không tên**. Each uses four localized observations, a documented cross-room comparison, physical anchor(s), two choices of inference and Core confirmation. These are rules of gameplay, not magical claims about LS-2.

The player's mandatory route remains:
`0.7 → dullness → epsilon → ls_2 → manila_room`.

LS-2 cannot be bypassed or completed through free-form GM narration. A Core-authorized action explicitly advances to Manila Room only after every authored LS-2 environment has been visited and the two puzzle chains verified.
