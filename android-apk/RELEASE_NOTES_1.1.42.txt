Backroom 1.1.42

Survival HUD and chat readability release.

- Fresh New Game now starts with known satisfied food, water, and rest baselines instead of showing those values as unknown.
- Keeps genuinely unknown physiology unknown for migrated characters or saves that do not establish those values.
- Adds continuous derived percentages for food, water, and rest without persisting duplicate state.
- Replaces the three plain hunger/thirst/sleep rows with futuristic survival HUD bars using meat, water, and Zzz indicators.
- Makes Character Detail Equipment and Inventory entries uppercase for faster scanning.
- Gives Game Master messages a slightly brighter background than Player messages for clearer visual separation.
- After each new Game Master response, the chat viewport moves to the beginning of that response instead of the bottom.
- Preserves existing Party, Status, Equipment, Inventory, save migration, game-time, and LiteRT behavior.
